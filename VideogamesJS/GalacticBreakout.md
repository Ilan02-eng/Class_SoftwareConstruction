**Galactic Breakout**  
Welcome to “Galactic Breakout”, an arcade challenge set in the reaches of the galaxy. Command your ship, deflect the coming asteroid and destroy the cosmic barriers before they overwhelm you. 

How to Play:

* **Move the Ship:** Use “left” and “right” arrow keys to move the ship.  
* **Launch:** Press the “space” key to release the asteroid to start playing or after losing a life and to restart the game. 

Rules:

* You have 3 lives, don’t let the asteroid fall or you’ll lose a life. When you lose all 3 lives it’s over.  
* Move the ship to stop the asteroid from falling to infinite space and to launch it towards the cosmic barriers.   
* Use the asteroid to hit and destroy the cosmic barriers before they reach you.  
* Every 15 seconds a new row of barriers will be added, pushing the existing ones down.  
* Every time you pass a level the next one starts with an extra row of cosmic barriers.   
* If the cosmic barriers reach the bottom of the screen (the ship’s level), it is an automatic GAME OVER.  
* Destroying cosmic barriers gives you points. 

Objective:  
Destroy the cosmic barriers as soon as possible to keep your ship safe from them, survive the constant descent, aim for the highest score and HAVE FUN\!
